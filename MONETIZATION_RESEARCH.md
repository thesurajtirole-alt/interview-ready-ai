# MONETIZATION_RESEARCH.md

Research date: 2026-09-17. Based on real web search results (cited),
not invented figures. Note: InterviewReady AI is a mock-interview +
training + coaching product, closer to Yoodli's category than to
Final Round AI's (which is primarily a live "copilot" that assists
during a real interview — a fundamentally different, more
controversial category this product should NOT position itself in).

## FACT — Competitor pricing found

**Yoodli** (closest category match — AI mock interview + speech
coaching):
- Free: 5 lifetime roleplay sessions (not monthly — total, ever)
- Pro: $8/month, billed annually only
- Advanced: $20/month, billed annually only, adds data-exclusion from
  AI training
- Enterprise: custom
- 2026 addition: candidates can paste a JD and get targeted questions
  generated for that role (directly comparable to this product's
  blueprint feature)

**Final Round AI** (different category — primarily a live interview
"copilot," not a practice tool):
- Reported figures vary significantly by source ($25-150/month
  depending on billing cycle and which article), monthly plan
  consistently cited around $148-150/month, annual around $25-50/month
  equivalent
- Documented user complaints: copilot freezing mid-interview, strict
  no-refund policy
- Not a close positioning match for this product — Final Round AI's
  core value prop (real-time answers during a live interview) is a
  different, ethically murkier category than mock-interview practice

**Other one-time-pack competitors observed** (from search results,
not directly verified against official pricing pages): one-time
packages in the $7-40 range for a fixed period of access, positioned
as cheaper alternatives to subscription-based "copilot" tools.

## INFERENCE

- The AI mock-interview/coaching category (Yoodli's category) prices
  meaningfully lower than the "live copilot" category (Final Round
  AI's category) — $8-20/month vs $25-150/month. This product belongs
  in the lower-priced category by function.
- Annual-only billing on paid tiers (as Yoodli does) is common in this
  space, likely because low-touch subscription products have thin
  margins on monthly billing after payment processing fees.
- A hard session cap (Yoodli's "5 lifetime sessions") is a common and
  apparently viable free-tier limiting mechanism — precedent for this
  product's own usage-limit approach.

## RECOMMENDATION

Given zero starting budget and the free-tier constraints already
documented in `AI_ARCHITECTURE.md` (Gemini's free tier: ~500
requests/day, ~15/minute at time of writing):

1. **Free tier**: limited by session count, not a paywall — e.g. 1 full
   mock interview + 3 training drills per rolling 30 days, matching the
   product's actual AI cost structure (a full interview costs ~18
   Gemini calls). This is directly enforceable today with zero
   payment infrastructure.
2. **Pricing model, once ready to charge**: a single paid tier in the
   $8-15/month range (undercutting Yoodli's Pro tier while offering
   comparable or better scope — panel simulation and real feedback
   loop are not present in Yoodli), billed monthly to start (annual-only
   billing can come later once there's confidence in retention).
3. **Do not compete on Final Round AI's positioning** ("live answers
   during your real interview") — both because it's a different,
   more ethically fraught product category, and because this
   product's actual value (practice + feedback + training) is
   better served by clear, honest positioning against Yoodli-style
   competitors.
4. **This recommendation is a starting point, not a final decision** —
   real pricing decisions should wait until there's actual usage data
   on how many free-tier sessions a typical engaged user consumes.
