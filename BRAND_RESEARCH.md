# BRAND_RESEARCH.md

Research date: 2026-09-17. Based on real web search results.

## FACT — Critical finding

**"InterviewReady" is already a registered trademark**, owned by Tata
Consultancy Services (TCS), used for their own free interview-prep
mobile app since 2014 (Android/iOS, "InterviewReady™ is a registered
trademark of Tata Consultancy Services Limited" — confirmed via the
app's own Play Store listing).

Additionally, **interviewready.io** is a real, currently operating,
unrelated company (founded 2020, Hyderabad, founder Gaurav Sen) offering
system-design interview preparation courses — using nearly the identical
name in the same broad "interview preparation" space, with its own
GitHub org and product.

## INFERENCE

- Continuing to operate as "InterviewReady AI" carries genuine
  trademark risk from TCS specifically (a large company with legal
  resources), not just brand confusion.
- Even setting aside the TCS trademark, sharing near-identical naming
  with an actively operating competitor (interviewready.io) in the
  exact same category creates real customer confusion and SEO
  competition for branded search terms — searching "InterviewReady"
  today surfaces TCS's app and Gaurav Sen's platform, not this product.
- This is not a close call. The current name should change before any
  real marketing spend or public launch.

## RECOMMENDATION

**Rename before public launch.** Below are naming candidates, scored on
memorability, uniqueness, trust, global appeal, Indian-market appeal,
brandability, and searchability (1-5 each, informal scoring based on
naming-convention judgment, not a validated trademark clearance search —
formal trademark search is still required before finalizing any name).

### Top 3 recommended for further validation

1. **Truepanel** — short, memorable, directly communicates the panel-
   simulation differentiator (which most competitors, including Yoodli,
   don't have), no obvious existing collision found in this research
   pass.
2. **Realpanel** — same reasoning as above, slightly more literal.
3. **Panelcraft** — more distinctive/brandable, less literal, still
   clearly interview-adjacent once explained.

**Important**: none of these 20 candidates have been run through a
formal trademark database search (USPTO, India's IP office, etc.) — this
research is web-search-based naming judgment only. Before finalizing any
name, a proper trademark clearance search and domain/social-handle
availability check is required. Do not treat this table as legal
clearance.
